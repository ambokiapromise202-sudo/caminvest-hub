Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a physical university?

No. We are an online-first business education platform. A physical campus is a future ambition, not current reality.

How do I enroll?

Click Enroll, register/login in Student Portal, you will be enrolled. Currently using secure local storage. Firebase backend required for production persistence.

Do you give certificates?

Yes, after completing lessons, quizzes and assignments, you receive a course completion certificate.
